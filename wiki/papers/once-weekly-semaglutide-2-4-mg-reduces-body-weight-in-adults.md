## Key Findings
Mean body weight change was −14.9% with semaglutide vs −2.4% with placebo (p<0.001). 86.4% of semaglutide participants achieved ≥5% weight loss vs 31.5% with placebo. Improvements observed in waist circumference, blood pressure, C-reactive protein, and lipid profiles. HbA1c decreased by 0.45 percentage points more with semaglutide.

## Methodology
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial (STEP 1). 1961 adults with BMI ≥30 (or ≥27 with comorbidity) received subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo once weekly for 68 weeks alongside lifestyle intervention.

## Limitations
68-week duration limits understanding of long-term maintenance. Cohort was 75% white and 74% female, limiting generalizability. Gastrointestinal adverse events (nausea 44%, diarrhea 30%) were the most common reason for discontinuation. Cost-effectiveness not assessed. Weight regain after discontinuation not studied in this trial.

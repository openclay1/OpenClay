How it works

Private AI in three steps

OpenClay connects your local AI engine with persistent memory and a research-grade context layer. No data ever leaves your machine.

01
💾

Install locally

Download OpenClay and Ollama. The setup script pulls the best model for your hardware automatically — works on Mac, Linux, and Windows.

02
🧠

Clay learns you

Upload documents, connect folders, set your SOUL.md profile. Clay builds a persistent memory of your work, preferences, and projects — all stored on your drive.

03
⚡

Research at depth

Ask questions, analyze FDA filings, run multi-agent chains, execute code. Every response draws from your private memory — no token limits on what Clay knows about you.

Capabilities

Everything you need, nothing sent out

🌐

Multilingual

Responds in Spanish or English automatically based on what you write.

📋

Document analysis

Upload PDFs, regulatory filings, and research papers. Clay reads, cites, and flags gaps.

🧰

5 specialist agents

Switch between General, Investigator, Clinician, Explorer, and Coder agents — each tuned differently.

⚙

Agentic tasks

Assign multi-step autonomous tasks. Clay plans, executes, and reports back asynchronously.

📈

Persistent memory

Mem0 + Hindsight layers capture facts, sessions, and inferred goals across every conversation.

🛠

Code sandbox

Execute Python, JS, and Bash locally in a sandboxed environment. Results shown on-canvas.

🎤

Voice I/O

Speak your questions with microphone input. Clay responds in your language via speech synthesis.

🔗

Folder watchers

Connect local folders for auto-ingestion. New files are indexed and available to Clay instantly.
